💪 Why learn push-ups?

Push-ups are a body-weight exercise that builds muscular strength and endurance. They help us practice pushing movements while keeping our bodies stable.

Chest

Pectoral muscles help push your body away from the wall or floor.

Back of arms

Triceps help straighten your elbows.

Shoulders

Shoulder muscles help control and support the movement.

Core

Abdominal and other trunk muscles help keep your body aligned.

Everyone's starting point is different. The goal is to practice safely and improve your own technique and strength.

🚀 Explore the five variations

Choose a variation that lets you practice with control. When you demonstrate 5 consecutive repetitions with proper form, you can try the next challenge. You can always return to an easier variation for additional practice.

1

Wall Push-Ups

Place your palms on a sturdy wall around shoulder height. Step back slightly, keep a straight body, bend your elbows, and push away.

🔍 Form Detective

Watch a demonstration and look for our three learning keys:

① Straight body

Keep your head, shoulders, hips, and supporting lower body aligned. Avoid sagging or bending at the hips.

② Bend your elbows

Use a controlled, comfortable range of motion. Avoid tiny, rushed repetitions.

③ Hands under shoulders

For floor push-ups, place hands approximately under shoulders; for wall push-ups, place palms around shoulder height.

📈 How to get stronger

1. Find your starting point

Choose a variation where you can practice with safe, controlled form.

2. Practice in short sets

Try several manageable sets with rest between them. Stop when your form breaks down.

3. Practice consistently

Our challenge aims for three practice days each week, with recovery days between sessions.

4. Celebrate progress

Record your best quality repetitions. Five consecutive correct reps earns an opportunity to try another variation.

At school, we will practice during supervised fitness blocks when a safe surface and suitable equipment are available. At home, ask an adult to help choose a safe practice area.
